Project Description

Objective: 
Develop a traditional Digital Image Processing (DIP) system to detect and measure visible cracks in concrete surfaces.

Motivation: 
Manual crack inspection in buildings, bridges, and roads can be time-consuming and dependent on human observation.

Key Techniques:
Grayscale Conversion, Black-Hat Transform, Thresholding, Morphological Filtering, Skeletonization, Pixel-Based Measurement.
